Policy ledger

Gross costs and offsets are not the same thing.

Different models answer different questions. Tariff estimates measure consumer purchasing-power loss. Tax-law estimates depend heavily on the baseline used. Benefit and healthcare changes are concentrated among affected households, so averaging them across everyone can be misleading.

What the tariff number means

It is a consumer-welfare estimate, not a customs receipt divided by households.

The Budget Lab models how tariffs change consumer prices, substitution, output and incomes. Its current-law estimate is about a 0.7% ultimate consumer-price impact and roughly $1,100 per household annually.
