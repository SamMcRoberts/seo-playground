Down-payment race

Waiting can cost more than PMI.

Each scenario lets the target home price move while the family saves. Lifetime cash outlay includes down payment, 30 years of principal and interest, modeled PMI, and closing costs. It excludes property taxes, homeowners insurance and maintenance because those continue regardless of down-payment size.
