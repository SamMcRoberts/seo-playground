True tax burden · broad economic incidence

Your tax bill is bigger than the number on your 1040.

Income tax is only one layer. A working family also bears payroll taxes, state and local taxes, property and sales taxes, excise taxes, and some taxes that are paid upstream but ultimately land on workers, consumers or investors.

Why paycheck math misses it

Visible taxes are not the whole economic burden.

There is no perfectly observable “true” tax incidence. A defensible measure must state who it assumes bears taxes that are legally remitted by employers and corporations.

FICA

Employer share

Employer payroll taxes reduce the compensation pool available to labor over time. ITEP includes employer-paid FICA in its income framework.

25%

Corporate tax to labor

ITEP follows a congressional-style long-run approach in assigning part of corporate income tax to labor, with most of the remainder assigned to owners of capital.
