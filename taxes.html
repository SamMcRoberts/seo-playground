Method & limits

Incidence-aware, not fake precision.

2026 federal income tax

Married-filing-jointly 2026 brackets, $32,200 standard deduction, and modeled $2,200 Child Tax Credit per qualifying child, subject to the usual eligibility rules.

Federal hidden taxes

Employer payroll is calculated under 2026 Social Security/Medicare law. Federal excise defaults to CBO’s 0.6% middle-quintile incidence benchmark and covers the household burden from federal fuel, aviation, alcohol, tobacco and other excise taxes as a bundle. Federal corporate incidence uses a conservative 0.8% benchmark.

No double counting

The ITEP state/local total already includes fuel tax, home/renter/car property tax, vehicle registration, driver's-license charges, selective excise taxes, business taxes, and many small licenses. The separate government-fees input is only for non-tax charges outside that model. Tariffs remain separate federal customs duties.
